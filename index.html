📍 Where is my History file? (detected: Windows)

Chrome keeps your browsing history in one file named History (no extension). On Windows it lives here:

  1. Copy it somewhere safe first.
  2. Click “Open Chrome History file” above.
  3. Choose the copy of History (for example the one on your Desktop).

Use more than one Chrome profile? Replace Default in the path with Profile 1, Profile 2, and so on. Everything is read inside your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Pick your Chrome History file to begin. The guide above shows exactly where it is on your computer.

Color = category, size = your Size-by choice, lines = the order you browsed. Faded, deeper dots are pages with little or no recorded time on page (adjust with Focus threshold).
